# MRIA Education

The public, bilingual entrance to MRIA Education: a rhizome-first relational
formation ecosystem for the Human + AI era.

## Public experience

- Interactive 3D Rhizome gate with an accessible list alternative
- Adaptive, active, facilitated, and embodied learning architecture
- Five open formation pathways and a Seven-Movement Formation Walk
- Relational Intake with no account, score, storage, or submission
- Programs, educator, institutional partnership, trust, and MRIA pages

The deployed landing is intentionally anonymous. Private Walk notes remain in
the visitor's page memory and may be printed or downloaded locally; they are not
transmitted to MRIA.

Confirmed courses, cohorts, schedules, facilitators, registration, credentials,
team profiles, partner case studies, and persistent learner services will be
published in later releases.
